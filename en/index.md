### ALM4GEST

An independently developed score management app for KONAMI's music games — "SOUND VOLTEX" and "DanceDanceRevolution" in the first release.

- Import your play data from e-amusement and browse scores, clear status, and EX scores in one list
- See what you are good at and where you fall short, through T-scores and tier lists built from all-player statistics
- Keep a record of your progress with growth charts and shareable images

This app is an unofficial tool developed and operated by an individual.
It is not provided or operated by Konami Arcade Games Co., Ltd. or any KONAMI group company, and has no affiliation with KONAMI.
